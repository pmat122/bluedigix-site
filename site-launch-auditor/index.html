Check a website before you launch it.

Site Launch Auditor is a website QA tool from Blue Digix Labs that reviews public websites for common launch-readiness problems and returns a prioritized verdict.

Built for ChatGPT

Crawl & indexing signals

Checks robots.txt, sitemap availability, meta robots, HTTPS, and public reachability.

Canonicals & redirects

Reviews canonical configuration and treats intentional external redirect endpoints separately from content pages.

Page metadata

Checks titles, descriptions, H1s, viewport metadata, Open Graph URL, and structured-data syntax.

Launch-readiness triage

Separates blockers, warnings, opportunities, and ignored/external findings so low-priority polish is not confused with launch failures.

When to use it

  • Before publishing a new website or moving a staging site into production.
  • After a deployment when you want a quick technical QA pass.
  • Before sending paid traffic to a new site.
  • When checking whether basic crawl and indexing signals are configured as expected.
  • When reviewing redirects, canonicals, metadata, or structured-data syntax after a site change.

What the audit returns

BLOCKER — a condition that can materially prevent a normal launch or intended crawling/indexing.

WARNING — a meaningful issue that deserves review but does not necessarily prevent launch.

OPPORTUNITY — lower-priority optimization or polish.

IGNORED / EXTERNAL — intentionally excluded items such as valid redirects to third-party destinations.

Important limits

Site Launch Auditor uses automated checks against publicly accessible resources. It does not render every JavaScript-driven interaction, submit forms, perform a complete accessibility review, certify legal or security compliance, guarantee search-engine indexing or rankings, or guarantee conversion performance. A capped crawl can also miss pages that exist elsewhere on a larger site.
